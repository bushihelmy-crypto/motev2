# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized attention, trolling, and publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy, and take proportionate corrective action, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
